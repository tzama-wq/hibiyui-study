Tiny Puzzle BGM Loops Vol.1
===========================
10 calm, seamless background-music loops for puzzle, cozy and casual games.

Files
- wav/  44.1 kHz 16-bit stereo, loops exactly (tails wrap into the start)
- ogg/  Vorbis q6, same loops, smaller for web/mobile
- loops.json  BPM, bar count, length and mood for each loop
- loopgen.py  the generator. Change BPM, chords or seeds and re-render.

How it was made
Every note and every sound is generated by a Python script (numpy):
sine pads, FM bells, Karplus-Strong plucks, a simple marimba, synthesized
kick/hat/tick. No samples, no sample libraries and no generative-AI music
models were used, so there are no third-party rights inside.
